Detailed Description

Implements a nuclear form factor based on Phys. Rev. 104, 1466 (1956). Parameter values are taken J. Cosmol. Astropart. Phys. 4, 012 (2007) which itself took fit results from Astropart. Phys. 6, 87 (1996)
